# USMLE gaps: neurology and GI

This lists commonly tested USMLE Step 1 facts and topics that the neurology and GI pages do not yet cover. It was made by checking every page against a list of high-yield facts per topic, searching the page text, and checking each miss by hand. Facts that a page already gives in other words (for example, 5-ASA for mesalazine) are not listed.

Treat it as a to-do list, not a source. Each item still needs checking against PubMed Central, StatPearls, and your coursework before it goes on a page.
